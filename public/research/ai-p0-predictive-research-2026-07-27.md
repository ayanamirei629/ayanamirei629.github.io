## Prediction-in-the-unknown contract

- A decision is formed using information available at the end of a session.
- Any hypothetical execution begins no earlier than the next tradable session.
- Training labels must be mature before they can be used.
- Chronological validation retains purge and embargo controls.
- Promotion requires a frozen rule and a sufficiently long forward evaluation period.
- Return, drawdown, predictive quality and data integrity must pass simultaneously.

## Current bottlenecks

1. The AI-era sample contains too few bear, panic and rebound episodes to validate timing across all market states.
2. Point-in-time stock-universe and delisting coverage remain incomplete.
3. The five-day stock model has not demonstrated useful out-of-sample rank information.
4. Multiple-testing and post-selection risk remain material for high-return candidates.
