04 · Research engineering

A repeatable system, not a one-off notebook.

The work combines quantitative research, data engineering, risk analysis and public communication. Private signal logic is excluded; the engineering contract and evidence discipline are shown here.

01 · INPUT

Data & lineage

Market, sentiment and cross-sectional inputs with coverage and staleness audits.

02 · CONTEXT

State diagnostics

Descriptive market context remains separate from tradeable outcome probabilities.

03 · LAB

Hypothesis families

Candidate families change one mechanism at a time and retain the baseline.

04 · GATES

Causal evaluation

Matured labels, embargoes, matched benchmarks, drawdown and multiplicity checks.

05 · OUTPUT

Governed artifacts

Versioned CSV/JSON evidence, shadow decisions and public-safe HTML reports.

TIMING_CONTRACT

Causal execution

Signals form after the close; returns begin only after the next tradable execution window.

POINT_IN_TIME

Data integrity

Incomplete historical membership and delisting evidence can block an otherwise profitable rule.

WALK_FORWARD

Selection discipline

Candidate selection uses only labels matured at each decision, with purge and embargo controls.

RISK_FIRST

Drawdown analysis

Initial NAV, phase resets, recovery paths, costs and stress cases are explicit reporting choices.

MULTIPLE_TESTS

Evidence governance

Benchmark wins do not promote a rule unless prediction, data and multiple-testing gates also pass.

REPORT_PIPELINE

Decision communication

Machine-readable audits and human-readable reports share the same result definitions.

06 · Public boundary

Enough evidence to evaluate the work—not enough to copy it.

This case study is designed for professional review. It exposes falsifiable results, limitations and research decisions while keeping the strategy implementation private.
